# Welcome to Resumaker

We built Resumaker because we were frustrated. Every resume builder we tried was built for the American market — left-to-right, English-only, oblivious to the quirks of Israeli HR.

So we built our own.

## What Makes Resumaker Different

**Native Hebrew support.** Not an afterthought. Every template is RTL-first, every font is optimized for Hebrew text, and every feature works in both Hebrew and English.

**ATS scoring out of the box.** Israeli companies increasingly use Applicant Tracking Systems (ATSs) to filter resumes before a human ever sees them. Resumaker scores your resume against the job description and tells you exactly which keywords are missing.

**Claude-powered generation.** We use Anthropic's Claude — one of the world's most capable language models — to tailor your resume to each specific job posting. Paste a description, get a resume. In under three seconds.

**Privacy-first.** Your resume content is yours. We don't sell it, we don't share it, and we don't train models on it. Period.

## Who It's For

Resumaker is built for:

- **Israeli tech workers** applying to local startups, multinationals, or abroad
- **Career changers** who need to reframe their experience for a new industry
- **New immigrants (Olim)** navigating a new job market with a different language
- **Students** writing their first professional resume
